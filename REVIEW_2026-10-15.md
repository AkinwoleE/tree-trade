## False positives / reclassified
- **Claude keeper H2 / Move #13 "bad debt beyond margin"**: not possible; close caps the loss at margin and liquidation takes exactly the margin. The real harm of missed liquidations is a free option (H-4), still High.
- **Claude Move #3 "the haircut leaves ~0.7% of profit at a 0.1% move"**: wrong. The on-chain formula keeps ~76% (PoC), so stale-price arbitrage is High, not Medium.
- **"Hyperliquid" in the UI**: none. Checked all pages and the 2.9 MB prod JS bundle; the API labels the source "market feed".
- **Vault invariant break**: none found. Both reviewers traced every path, and `review_tests::invariant_through_queue_and_debt` checks `vault == balances + margin + pool + dev_fees` through queue, debt opens, liquidation and crank. The queue and debt are unbacked liabilities by design.
